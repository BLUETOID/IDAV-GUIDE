03 / A useful way to revise

Read. Draw. Explain.

  1. Understand the topic. Read the definition, the assumptions and a worked example in the unit notes.
  2. Recreate the diagram. Use the labelled figures to explain the process or model in your own words.
  3. Answer from memory. Open the linked short or long answer and compare it with your attempt.
